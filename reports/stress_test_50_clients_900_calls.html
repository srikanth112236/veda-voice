What Was Tested & How It Was Executed

  • Multi-Tenant Concurrency: Seeded 50 isolated tenants across 10 industries with active Pro subscriptions, dedicated DIDs, returning caller contacts, strict grounding, objection playbooks, and auto-quote flags in an isolated temporary SQLite database.
  • Simultaneous 10 Inbound + 8 Outbound per Client (900 Calls): Executed via asyncio.gather and httpx.AsyncClient(ASGITransport(app)) across 64 concurrent async workers.
  • Outbound Compliance Gate: Every single one of the 400 outbound calls passed through evaluate_compliance_gate (consent, calling window, DNC check) prior to /calls/start.
  • Full Call Lifecycle: Each call executed /api/v1/internal/calls/start (call-scoped JWT) → /caller-context (returning caller memory) → /calendar/availability → /quotes/send-link (50 mid-call quotes) → /calls/complete (transcript, CRM contact upsert, post-call auto-quote).
  • Tenant Isolation Audit: Verified each of the 50 tenants persisted exact 18 calls (10 inbound + 8 outbound) and zero cross-tenant bleed.
